TUTOR NOTES

Order (easy to hard, one new thing per game): 1 watch the 5 moves (3-digit ÷ 1-digit) · 2 do them · 3 remainders · 4 first digit too small, 4-digit ÷ 1-digit · 5 zero in the answer · 6 build a ladder for a 2-digit divisor · 7 3-digit ÷ 2-digit · 8 4-digit ÷ 2-digit · 9 find the bug · 10 boss.

Moves: Divide (drag the ladder rung to the top), Multiply (drag the rung's product down), Subtract (type), Check (is the leftover smaller than the divisor?), Bring down (drag the digit down with the mouse). Say the moves out loud each loop.

Tutor keys: press B (outside a text box) for a hidden 5-minute break timer. Shift-click a locked game on the map to open it.

Science: worked example first, then fading (Sweller; Renkl). Aim for about 85% right (Wilson et al., 2019). Bonus diamonds are a 50% coin flip after each win; stars are never taken away. Tap the logo for the map; any game can be opened from it.
